Privacy Policy — Sunique Accounting Automation

Effective 2026-10-16

Who we are. Sunique International ("Sunique", "we") operates the application "Sunique Accounting Automation" (the "App"). The App is an internal tool used only by Sunique's own accounting staff and automation to work with Sunique's own QuickBooks Online companies. It is not offered to the public and has no users outside Sunique.

What the App accesses. With the authorization of a Sunique administrator, the App reads accounting records from Sunique's QuickBooks Online companies (customers, vendors, invoices, bills, payments, accounts and reports) and, where enabled, creates or updates accounting records on Sunique's behalf. The App does not access QuickBooks companies that do not belong to Sunique.

How data is used. Data is used solely for Sunique's bookkeeping, accounts receivable, accounts payable, reconciliation and management reporting. It is not sold, rented or used for advertising.

Service providers. Sunique may process this data with service providers acting on its behalf: Microsoft Azure (hosting, United States) and AI assistant services from Anthropic and OpenAI, which Sunique staff use to classify, match and reconcile records under human review. Each provider processes the data under its own service terms; Sunique does not permit any other use of it. No other third party receives the data.

Where data is kept. Data is processed on Sunique-controlled computers in the United States and on Sunique's Microsoft Azure resources in the United States (South Central US). Access tokens are stored encrypted or in access-restricted files available only to Sunique administrators.

Retention. Exported working files are kept only as long as needed for the accounting period they support and are deleted afterwards. Authorization can be revoked at any time from QuickBooks Online (Apps → Connected apps) or by Sunique's administrator.

Security. Access is limited to named Sunique accounts; credentials are never shared by e-mail or chat; the App never initiates payments or changes bank details.

Contact. accounting@suniquecabinetry.com

Changes. This policy may be updated; the effective date above changes when it is.
